Our Story

Built from the Ground Up in the Oil Field

Time Ahead Constructions was founded with a single goal: to provide the oil & gas sector with a contractor that holds safety and quality as non-negotiable. Starting with small piping packages in Kerala, we have grown into a multi-discipline mechanical contractor trusted by major refineries.

Our core team brings decades of hands-on experience from BPCL, HPCL, and ONGC projects. Today we operate with 500+ trained personnel and a track record of zero lost-time incidents.
