HSE Commitment

Zero Harm. Every Site. Every Day.

Safety is not a procedure — it is our culture. We operate to the highest HSE standards on every project, protecting our people, clients, and the environment.

Our Safety Framework

How We Keep Every Worker Safe

A structured, proactive approach to HSE management across all operations.

🧹

Personal Protective Equipment

Mandatory PPE protocols for every role — helmets, gloves, safety boots, eye protection, and FR clothing as required by task.

📄

Permit to Work (PTW)

Strict PTW system for all hot work, confined space entry, height work, and electrical isolation. No work begins without authorised permits.

🔒

LOTO — Lockout / Tagout

Comprehensive energy isolation procedures for all maintenance and shutdown activities, preventing accidental equipment energisation.

🕨

Confined Space Entry

Trained supervisors, gas testing, standby rescue teams, and continuous atmospheric monitoring for all confined space operations.

🤓

Safety Harness & Fall Protection

100% tie-off policy for all work at height above 1.8m. Inspected harnesses, certified anchorage, and safety officers on site.

🔥

Hot Work Controls

Gas-free certification, fire watch, fire extinguisher standby, and area isolation for all welding, cutting, and grinding activities.

🕑

Daily Toolbox Talks

Every shift begins with a task-specific toolbox talk — hazard identification, method review, and team briefing before work commences.

📋

Job Safety Analysis (JSA)

Written JSA prepared for every non-routine task, reviewed with the crew, and kept on file with the shift supervisor.

🎓

Safety Training & Inductions

All personnel undergo site induction, HSE orientation, and role-specific training before being deployed on any client facility.
